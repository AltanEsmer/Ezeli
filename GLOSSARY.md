# Glossary

- **Version 1**: Mazi as built (the `dodoscoding/Mazi` repo). Judged a failure; not carried forward.
- **Version 2**: the restart, built in this repo. Its first release, not a later one.
- **Memory**: a moment from the group's chats: the photos sent close together, plus the messages around them. Not a single photo (that was version 1's meaning).
- **Person**: a member of the friend group, known by the name they send under in the chats.
- **Chat**: one WhatsApp group or Discord channel whose history is loaded into the app.
- **Export**: the files an owner gets out of a chat app for one chat: the messages and the media sent in it.
- **Import**: loading a WhatsApp or Discord export into the app. Done only by the owners, never by friends.
- **Comment**: a note a person adds to a memory inside the app.
- **Owners**: Altan and Dodo. They decide together; Altan has the final say.
- **Ezeli**: the product's name from version 2 onward. Mazi is the name of version 1 only.
- **Personal link**: the secret link an owner sends one friend privately. Opening it signs that friend in as themselves; no email or password.
